SNOWBOARDING

Snowboarding is a recreational and competitive activity that involves descending a mountain while standing on a snowboard with bindings securing the rider's feet to the board. Almost like a snow version of skateboarding.

Reece Catalogo - 12/23/2025

FREESTYLE SKIING

Skiing is the use of skis to glide on snow for basic transport, a recreational activity, or a competitive winter sport. Many types of competitive skiing events are recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), and the International Ski and Snowboard Federation (FIS).

Reece Catalogo - 03/10/2026

SNOWSCOOT

Snowscooting, or snow biking, is a winter sport that emerged in the 1990s, inspired by BMX, and is practiced by gliding on snow using handlebars. It is practiced in the same environment as alpine skiing or snowboarding, in ski resorts or off-piste, and in all snow conditions.

OLYMPICS

February 4 - 22

The 2026 Winter Olympics, officially called Milano Cortina 2026, were the 25th Winter Olympic Games, held in northern Italy from February 6 to February 22, 2026. The Games were jointly hosted by the cities of Milan and Cortina d’Ampezzo, making them the first Winter Olympics officially co-hosted by two cities.

X GAMES

January 23 - 25

The X Games Aspen 2026 was a major winter action-sports competition held January 23–25, 2026, at Buttermilk Mountain in Aspen. The event brought together many of the world’s top athletes in extreme winter sports to compete for X Games medals.

REDBULL NATURAL SELECTION

March 10 - 15

Red Bull Natural Selection (also called the Natural Selection Tour) is a professional snowboarding competition that showcases the world’s best riders competing on natural mountain terrain rather than traditional terrain parks.
